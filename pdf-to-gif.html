Convert PDF to animated GIF

Turn the pages of a PDF into an animated GIF slideshow, with your own speed and size. Private and in your browser.

How to: pdf to gif

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Choose the speed, width and which pages to include.
  3. Click “Create GIF” and download it.

About this pdf to gif tool

Turn the pages of a PDF – slides, comics or storyboards – into an animated GIF. Control the seconds per page, width and quality and download the result.

Questions

Can I pick which pages go in?

Yes, type ranges like 1-3, 5 or leave it empty for every page.

Why is my GIF big?

GIFs are large by nature. Lower the width or the page count to shrink it.

How do I make the GIF smaller?

Reduce the width or the number of pages, or choose “Smaller file” quality.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional tip (1€, plus 1€ per 100 processed pages) is only a suggestion.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the tools are cached by your browser and keep working without an internet connection.
