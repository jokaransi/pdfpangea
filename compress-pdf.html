Compress PDF to a smaller size

Shrink PDF file size right in your browser. Choose a light lossless pass or stronger compression that re-renders pages as optimised images.

How to: compress pdf

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Pick a compression level.
  3. Download the smaller file – we keep the original if we can't improve it.

Questions

Will text stay selectable?

With “Light” yes. “Balanced” and “Strong” turn pages into images for much smaller files, so text is no longer selectable.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional donation (1 cent per processed page) is only a suggestion.
