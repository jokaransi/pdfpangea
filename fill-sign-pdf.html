Fill & sign PDF online, free

Fill out forms and contracts and sign them: place text, ticks, dates, your drawn signature or an image anywhere on the page. Your document and signature stay on your device.

How to: fill & sign

  1. Add a PDF – the pages appear as an editable sheet.
  2. Pick Text, Tick, Date, Signature or Image, then click the spot on the page where it should go.
  3. Drag to reposition entries, adjust text with the Size control, and resize images or signatures. Switch pages, then save the finished PDF.

About this fill & sign tool

See every page of your PDF as an editable sheet and click where something belongs: type text, tick a box, insert today's date, draw your signature or place an image such as a stamp. Drag entries to reposition them, adjust text with the Size control, and resize images or signatures.

It works on flat PDFs and scans too, so you can fill out forms, applications and contracts without printing. Nothing is uploaded – your document and signature never leave your device.

This places a visual signature on the page; it is not a certificate-based qualified electronic signature.

Questions

Can I fill out a PDF that has no form fields?

Yes. You can place text anywhere on the page, so scanned forms and flat PDFs work too.

Is this a legally qualified electronic signature?

No. It places a visual signature on the page, which is fine for most everyday paperwork. Documents that legally require a qualified electronic signature need a certified signing service.

Is my signature stored anywhere?

No. It only exists in your browser until you close or reload the page.

Can I move things after placing them?

Yes. Drag any entry to move it, drag the corner of a signature or image to resize it, and use ✕ to delete it.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional tip (1€, plus 1€ per 100 processed pages) is only a suggestion.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the tools are cached by your browser and keep working without an internet connection.
