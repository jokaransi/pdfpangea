Rotate PDF pages

Fix sideways scans by rotating all pages – or only selected ones – permanently.

How to: rotate pdf

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Choose the angle and optionally the pages.
  3. Download the rotated PDF.

About this rotate pdf tool

Fix sideways scans by rotating the whole PDF or only chosen pages by 90°, 180° or 270°. The new orientation is saved permanently in the file.

Questions

Can I rotate only some pages?

Yes, type the pages (e.g. 2-4) or leave empty for all.

Can I rotate only some pages?

Yes. Enter the page numbers or ranges, or leave the field empty to rotate every page.

Is the rotation permanent?

Yes, the downloaded PDF opens with the new orientation in any viewer.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional tip (€1 plus 1 cent per processed page) is only a suggestion.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the tools are cached by your browser and keep working without an internet connection.
