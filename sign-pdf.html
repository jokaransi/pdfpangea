Sign a PDF online for free

Draw your signature and add it to selected PDF pages. Your document and signature stay on your device.

How to: sign pdf

  1. Add a PDF and draw your signature with a finger, mouse or stylus.
  2. Choose pages to sign, or leave the field empty to sign every page.
  3. Download your signed PDF.

Questions

Where is the signature placed?

It is placed near the bottom-right of every selected page. The tool does not provide a legally qualified electronic signature.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional donation (€1 plus 1 cent per processed page) is only a suggestion.
