Privacy

All PDF and image processing happens inside your browser. Your files are not uploaded, transmitted or stored on any server.

What is stored

A single cookie (hp_donation) on your device remembers how many pages you processed and how much you chose to donate. It contains no personal data and is never required to use the tools. The site also caches its own files so it can work offline.
