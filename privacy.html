Privacy

All PDF and image processing happens inside your browser. Your files are not uploaded, transmitted or stored on any server.

What is stored

If you accept, a single cookie (hp_donation) is saved on your device. It remembers how many pages you processed and how much you chose to donate. It contains no personal data, is never used for tracking or advertising, and nothing is shared with anyone. There are no ads and no third-party cookies. The tools work the same if you decline; the counter then only lasts until you close the page. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage. You can change it any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer. The site also caches its own files so it can work offline.
