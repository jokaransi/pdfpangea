Extract pages from a PDF

Create a new PDF containing only the pages you pick. Page order follows what you type.

How to: extract pages

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Enter the pages you want, e.g. 1, 3-5.
  3. Download the new PDF.

About this extract pages tool

Pull a few pages out of a long document and save them as a new PDF. Great for sending only the relevant part of a contract, report or book chapter.

Questions

Can I reorder while extracting?

Yes – entering “3,1” puts page 3 before page 1.

What is the difference between extract and split?

Extract creates one new PDF from the selected pages; split cuts the document into several files.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional tip (1€, plus 1€ per 100 processed pages) is only a suggestion.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the tools are cached by your browser and keep working without an internet connection.
