Split a PDF into several files

Cut a PDF into separate documents by page ranges, every N pages or one file per page – privately, on your device.

How to: split pdf

  1. Add a PDF.
  2. Choose how to split: ranges like 1-3, 4-6, every N pages, or every page.
  3. Download each file or all of them as a ZIP.

About this split pdf tool

Break a large PDF into smaller documents. Split by custom page ranges such as 1-3, 5, 8-10, every N pages, or extract each page as its own file. Multiple results are bundled into a ZIP.

The splitter runs locally, so even sensitive PDFs stay on your device.

Questions

Can I split by custom ranges?

Yes – enter comma-separated ranges such as 1-3, 4-6, 7-. Each range becomes its own PDF.

How do I split a PDF by page range?

Choose “Custom ranges” and enter ranges like 1-3, 5, 8-10. Each range becomes its own PDF.

Can I get every page as a separate PDF?

Yes, choose “Every page”. You receive a ZIP with one PDF per page.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional tip (1€, plus 1€ per 100 processed pages) is only a suggestion.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the tools are cached by your browser and keep working without an internet connection.
