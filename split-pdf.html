Split a PDF into several files

Cut a PDF into separate documents by page ranges, every N pages or one file per page – privately, on your device.

How to: split pdf

  1. Add a PDF.
  2. Choose how to split: ranges like 1-3, 4-6, every N pages, or every page.
  3. Download each file or all of them as a ZIP.

Questions

Can I split by custom ranges?

Yes – enter comma-separated ranges such as 1-3, 4-6, 7-. Each range becomes its own PDF.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional donation (1 cent per processed page) is only a suggestion.
