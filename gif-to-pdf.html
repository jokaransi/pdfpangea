Convert animated GIF to PDF

Split an animated GIF into its frames and put them into a PDF – one page per frame. Everything stays on your device.

How to: gif to pdf

  1. Add your GIF.
  2. Choose page size and how many frames to keep.
  3. Click “Create PDF”.

Questions

Does a PDF animate?

No, a PDF cannot play animation. Each GIF frame becomes its own page.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional donation (€1 plus 1 cent per processed page) is only a suggestion.
