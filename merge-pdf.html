Merge PDF files online, free

Combine multiple PDF files into a single document in the order you want. It runs entirely in your browser – your files are never uploaded.

How to: merge pdf

  1. Add two or more PDF files.
  2. Drag them (or use the arrows) into the right order.
  3. Click “Merge PDFs” and download the result.

Questions

Is there a limit on files or size?

No fixed limit. Because everything runs on your device, the only limit is your computer's memory.

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. Merging happens locally in your browser.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional donation (1 cent per processed page) is only a suggestion.
