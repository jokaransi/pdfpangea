Merge PDF files online, free

Combine multiple PDF files into a single document in the order you want. It runs entirely in your browser – your files are never uploaded.

How to: merge pdf

  1. Add two or more PDF files.
  2. Drag them (or use the arrows) into the right order.
  3. Click “Merge PDFs” and download the result.

About this merge pdf tool

Need to merge PDF files without emailing them to a stranger's server? pdf pangea joins invoices, scans, contracts and chapters into one PDF right on your device. Reorder the files by drag and drop or with the arrow buttons and the merged document keeps all pages, links and quality.

Because the PDF merger works locally, it is as fast as your computer and works even when you are offline. There is no file size cap, no queue and no account.

Questions

Is there a limit on files or size?

No fixed limit. Because everything runs on your device, the only limit is your computer's memory.

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. Merging happens locally in your browser.

How do I combine PDF files into one?

Add your PDFs, arrange them in the order you want and press “Merge PDFs”. The combined file downloads instantly.

Does merging reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are, so text, images and fonts stay identical.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files are never uploaded. A small optional tip (1€, plus 1€ per 100 processed pages) is only a suggestion.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the tools are cached by your browser and keep working without an internet connection.
